Privacy first

We can't read your data. By design.

No accounts, no servers holding your health data, no ads, no third-party trackers.

🔒

Local-first

All health entries live only on your device — never in iCloud, never on any server. Even we can't access them.

🚫

No data selling

We never sell, rent, or share your data, and embed no advertising or tracking SDKs. Period.

📤

Export or back up anytime

Use raw CSV/PDF export, a clinician report, or a password-encrypted manual backup. Local records remain fully deletable — never paywalled.

Features

Built for real cycles — including irregular ones.

Maren learns your cycle instead of assuming "day 14" ovulation, so it works for 15–120 day cycles, PCOS, and everything in between.

📅

Calendar & phases

Track flow, see the four cycle phases color-coded, with honest confidence ranges — not fake precision.

🧠

Adaptive prediction

Statistical model that adapts to your history and shows a range, not a false "exact day".

😊

Mood & body signals

Log mood, energy, pain, sleep, weight, basal body temperature, spotting, notes, and custom items.

✨

Personal insights

On-device trends, recent-cycle comparison, and correlation exploration. Premium.

⏰

Smart reminders

Period, PMS self-care, and phase-aware nudges. Medication multi-time & weekday scheduling. Premium.

❤️

Apple Health

Choose menstrual flow, body mass, sleep, basal temperature, spotting, step count, or Apple Exercise Time. Sleep, steps, and exercise time are read-only; no Health data is sent to a developer server.

⌚

Watch, Widget & Shortcuts

Quick-log with App Shortcuts, an interactive widget, or the Apple Watch app and Smart Stack.

📄

Clinician report

Generate a structured PDF for a healthcare visit. Maren warns you that shared CSV/PDF files are not encrypted.

🔐

Encrypted manual backup

Export a password-encrypted backup, preview it before import, and choose safe merge or confirmed replacement.
