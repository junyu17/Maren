Features

Built for real cycles — including irregular ones.

Maren learns your cycle instead of assuming "day 14" ovulation, so it works for 15–120 day cycles, PCOS, and everything in between.

📅

Calendar & phases

Track flow, see the four cycle phases color-coded, with honest confidence ranges — not fake precision.

🧠

Adaptive prediction

Statistical model that adapts to your history and shows a range, not a false "exact day".

😊

Mood & symptoms

A 3-second daily check-in: mood, energy, pain, sleep, weight, and custom symptoms.

✨

Personal insights

On-device patterns — like which symptoms cluster in your luteal phase. Premium.

⏰

Smart reminders

Period, PMS self-care, and phase-aware nudges. Medication multi-time & weekday scheduling. Premium.

❤️

Apple Health

Optional two-way sync with Apple Health — read & write periods, only on your device.

Watch & Widget
