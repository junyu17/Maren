# Maren - Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 28, 2026**
**Contact: billy.yu@me.com**

Your health data is yours. Maren is built so that we never see it.

## The short version
- Your cycle, symptom, and mood entries are stored **on your device by default**. You may optionally turn on **iCloud sync (§2)** to sync them to your own private iCloud account across your devices.
- **We do not operate any server that stores your health data. We cannot read it.**
- **We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or health data with anyone.**
- **We do not use your data for advertising**, and we do not embed any third‑party advertising or tracking SDKs.
- You can **export all your data** (CSV/PDF) or **delete it** at any time - free, forever.

## 1. What data Maren handles and where it lives

| Data | Where it is stored | Who can access it |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle, period, symptom, mood, and related entries you log | Your device (local storage) by default; your private iCloud only if you enable sync (§2) | **Only you.** Not us. Not third parties. |
| App settings & preferences | Your device (and your iCloud if sync is on) | Only you |
| Purchase / subscription status | Apple (StoreKit) | Apple, and us (status only - no health data, no payment details) |

We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, or advertising identifier. Maren has no analytics and no user accounts.

## 2. iCloud sync (optional, off by default)
iCloud sync is **off by default**. If you turn it on in Settings, your data is stored in **your own iCloud account** using Apple's CloudKit **private** database, encrypted and synced across devices signed in to the same Apple ID. **Maren's developer has no access to your CloudKit private data** - only you and Apple can. Sync is entirely optional; with it off, your data never leaves your device except when you choose to export it. You can turn it off or delete your data at any time.

## 3. Apple Health (optional)
If you choose to connect Apple Health, Maren can read your period entries from Health (to import them) and write the periods you log in Maren back to Health (to share with other apps you use). This data is used **only** on your device to provide these features. It is **never** sent to us, **never** used for advertising, and **never** shared with third parties or sold. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and manage permissions in the Health app.

## 4. Purchases
Maren offers an optional subscription and one‑time purchase (Maren Premium), processed by **Apple** via StoreKit. We receive only your entitlement status (whether you have Premium). We never receive your name or payment details. Your logging and data export always remain free.

## 5. No analytics, no third parties
Maren contains **no analytics SDK and no third‑party tracking or advertising code**. The app makes no network calls with your health data except to **Apple's own services** that you explicitly enable: StoreKit (for purchases), optional iCloud (CloudKit) sync (to your own private iCloud - §2), and optional Apple Health (which stays on your device - §3). We have no server of our own that touches your health data.

## 6. What Maren is NOT
Maren is a **tracking and insight** tool for personal awareness. **It is not a contraceptive, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice or guarantees about fertility, ovulation, or pregnancy prevention.** Predictions are estimates and may be inaccurate, especially for irregular cycles. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

## 7. Children
Maren is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 8. Your rights & controls
At any time, from within the app, you can: view all your data, export it (CSV/PDF), and permanently delete it. Deleting the app removes local data; if iCloud sync was on, you can also delete the synced data from within the app or via iOS iCloud settings. Because we do not hold your data on our servers, you can fulfil most data‑subject requests (GDPR/CCPA access, deletion, portability) yourself, directly in the app. For questions, contact billy.yu@me.com.

## 9. Legal bases & regions
We process the minimal data described above to provide the service you request. This policy is designed to align with **GDPR (EEA/UK)** and **CCPA/CPRA (California)** principles. Where required, health data is treated as sensitive/special‑category data and is processed only on your device (and, if you enable it, your own private iCloud), with your consent.

## 10. Changes
We will post any changes here and update the effective date. Material changes will be surfaced in‑app.
